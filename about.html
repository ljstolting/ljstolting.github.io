About Me

Bio

I am currently a PhD candidate in Neuroscience and Cognitive Science at Indiana University, where I work in Dr. Randall Beer's Computational Neuroethology lab. My dissertation research focuses on the interplay between robustness and flexibility in neural circuits. I design and analyze minimal models of neuronal homeostasis, motor pattern generation, and neuromodulatory control to establish general principles of adaptability. At the center of these models are the concepts of degeneracy, embodiment, and dynamics. I also enjoy teaching and learning about pedagogy. Outside of work, I play tennis, write creatively, and hang out with my dog. I am seeking a postdoc position starting in Spring 2027 (expected defense date December 2026).
